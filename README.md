## 🌟 About the Artist

> "Saidus Salehin Khaled Sumon, universally celebrated across South Asia as **Bassbaba Sumon** (বেসবাবা সুমন), is a titan of Bangladeshi rock music, widely heralded as the pioneer of slap-bass techniques and modern rock bass guitar in the subcontinent. After electrifying tenures with legendary acts including Feelings and Warfaze, Sumon founded the trailblazing rock band **Aurthohin** in 1998, ushering in a golden era of progressive metal, funk-rock, and acoustic anthems. Beyond his band duties, Sumon has composed and produced a landmark body of solo and collaborative work that stands as some of the most influential, emotive, and technically revered music in modern Bengali history."

### 📖 About the Album
Released in 1997 by Soundtek, *Sumon O Aurthohin* ("Sumon & Meaningless") is Bassbaba Sumon's seminal debut solo album and the conceptual genesis of the band **Aurthohin**. Track #9, *"Aurthohin"*, resonated so profoundly that it inspired the name of the revolutionary band Sumon formed the following year. Filled with raw artistic energy, introspective acoustic ballads, and socially conscious lyrics, the record gave birth to immortal Bangladeshi rock standards including *"Tomar Jonno Noy"*, *"Mone Koro"*, *"Kono Ek Nijhum Raate"*, and *"Tahader Kotha '71"*.

### 📖 About the Album
Released in 2002 by G-Series under the artist credit *"Aurthohin Er Sumon"*, *Shopnogulo Tomar Moto* ("Dreams Like Yours") is a deeply emotional, cathartic solo milestone dedicated to the sacred memory of Aurthohin's late guitarist Rupok. Written, arranged, recorded, and mixed in an intense six days at Studio Bass with Iqbal Asif Jewel, the record captures visceral grief, poetic solace, and virtuosic musicality across immortal songs such as *"Rupoker Gaan"*, *"Austhirota"*, *"Aadhare"*, and the blistering slap-bass tour-de-force *"Funkedelic Fried Rice"*.

### 📖 About the Album
Released in 2007 by G-Series, *Ekhon Ami* ("Now It Is Me") is one of the most celebrated and commercially successful duet albums in modern Bengali music history. Bringing together Bassbaba Sumon's emotive vocal presence and songwriting genius with Anila's ethereal voice, the album produced massive cultural touchstones like *"Gaibona"*, *"Ekhon Ami"*, *"Shopnogulo Tomar Moto"*, and *"Noshto Neer"*. This release also features the Fuad Al-Muqtadir alternate mix of *"Shobar Jonne Tumi"*.

### 📖 About the Album
Released in October 2016 by Mushroom Entertainment, *Soul Food, Pt. 1* is Bassbaba Sumon's groundbreaking international instrumental bass record. Featuring world-renowned guest drummer Mark Don, the album is an awe-inspiring masterclass in solo bass guitar artistry — fusing lightning-fast percussive slap-and-pop techniques, intricate two-handed tapping, groove metal riffs, and jazz-funk harmonics. Written during Sumon's courageous recovery journey, tracks like *"Cancer Casserole"*, *"Tapping Tempura"*, and *"Chef Johns Ragadelic Baba Ghanoush"* stand as monumental testaments to human resilience and instrumental brilliance.
